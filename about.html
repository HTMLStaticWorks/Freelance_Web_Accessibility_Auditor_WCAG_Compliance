The Story Behind AccessAudit

I started as a front-end developer who realized that the web was being built for a narrow audience. Seeing how many barriers existed for users with disabilities, I shifted my focus to accessibility auditing.

With over 8 years of experience in the industry, I now help companies of all sizes navigate the complex landscape of WCAG, ADA, and Section 508 compliance.

Our Mission

"To ensure that digital experiences are a human right, not a privilege, by removing every technical barrier between people and information."
