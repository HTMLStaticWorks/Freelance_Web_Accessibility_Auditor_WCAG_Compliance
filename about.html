The Story Behind AccessAudit

I started as a front-end developer who realized that the web was being built for a narrow audience. Seeing how many barriers existed for users with disabilities, I shifted my focus to accessibility auditing.

With over 8 years of experience in the industry, I now help companies of all sizes navigate the complex landscape of WCAG, ADA, and Section 508 compliance.

Guiding Philosophy

Accessibility is a Journey, Not a Destination

Achieving WCAG compliance is a significant milestone, but true digital inclusion requires ongoing commitment. As the web evolves and your digital products grow, new barriers can unintentionally arise.

My goal is to empower your team to integrate accessibility into their daily workflows. By embedding inclusive design principles from the very start, I can prevent issues before they reach production and ensure a seamless experience for every single user, regardless of their abilities.

  • Shift-left testing approach
  • Developer and designer training
  • Long-term compliance monitoring

Methodology

My Auditing Approach

Combining automated tools with rigorous manual testing to ensure every user has a seamless experience.

1. Discovery & Scanning

I start by understanding your core user journeys and running automated scans to identify structural issues.

2. Manual AT Testing

Automated tools only catch 30% of issues. I manually navigate your site using screen readers and keyboard-only methods.

3. Actionable Reporting

You receive a detailed report with exact code snippets, screenshots, and prioritization to guide your developers.

4. Remediation Support

I provide ongoing support to your development team, verifying fixes and answering technical questions until compliance is achieved.
