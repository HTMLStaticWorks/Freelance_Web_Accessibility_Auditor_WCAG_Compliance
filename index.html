Our Roadmap

The Compliance Process

01. Discovery & Scope

We analyze your digital ecosystem and define the audit scope based on your business needs.

02. Technical Audit

Manual and automated testing across all key pages and user flows using assistive technology.

03. Reporting & VPAT

Detailed reports detailing violations, impact levels, and specific remediation code snippets.

04. Verification & Certification

Final re-testing after fixes to verify compliance and provide a certificate of accessibility.
