Audit FAQs

How long does a typical audit take?
For a medium-sized website (approx. 20-30 pages), a full technical audit typically takes between 10 to 15 business days.
Do you fix the code yourself?
We provide detailed remediation guidance and code snippets. While we don't usually implement the fixes directly on your production server, we can work closely with your dev team or offer remediation as a separate service.
What happens if we fail the audit?
An audit isn't a pass/fail test; it's a roadmap to compliance. We identify all barriers and help you fix them until your site meets the WCAG 2.1 AA standards.
